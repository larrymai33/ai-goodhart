# Coverage between audited budgets

This supporting research phase makes the declared-grid limitation concrete and
provides an exact deterministic transfer rule. It does not replace the original
seven-budget decision, improve its label-cost benchmark, or establish new
generic convexity theory. The user authorized continued offline research and
public publication; no new approval, download, model run, or outreach is needed.

For fixed nonnegative losses, prove discrete convexity of R_n/n directly by
Pascal's identity. Attribute the expected-minimum connection to Myatt and
Ronayne, Proposition 1, and distinguish Fitas's total-variation interpolation.
Derive the two-endpoint upper bound and the integer interval factor
floor((a+b)^2/4)/(a*b). Show the uniformly sharp 9/8 dyadic factor, including
the fractional original-instance witness and a separate binary 32-prompt
witness. A grid-level certificate at s transfers to all budgets at tau only
when the displayed factors permit it; this implication alone does not give
boundary completeness for a new full-family auditor.

Add dependency-free exact helpers for endpoint interpolation, the interval
factor, and a smallest grid under this sufficient criterion. The latter is
explicitly not a globally smallest grid under all possible inference rules.
Use integers and Fraction inputs, reject booleans/floats and out-of-domain
values, and cap pool size at 100,000 to bound output length. Bounds lie in
[0,1]. The grid requires 0<=s<=tau<=1 and includes 1 and m. Endpoint
interpolation accepts 1<=a<=n<=b with a<b and clamps the upper bound at one.

Use exact subset enumeration, discrete identities, and an independent shortest
path calculation to check the implementation. Preserve an internally specified
protocol and source freeze before the saved exhaustive run; reproduce its
bytes. The earlier floating LP was exploratory, not the source of the exact
certificate. Keep all earlier pinned files intact. Total added storage remains
well below 2 GB. Report the scientific scope and AI assistance plainly.

Builder decisions: a small pure Python module and a separate proof/check report;
no new CLI, annotation service, statistical test, or manuscript rebuild.
