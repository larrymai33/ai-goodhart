# Consolidated sharp-cost manuscript

## Scope

Create a self-contained, readable research manuscript for the existing v0.7 theorem and monotone-family counterexample. This is a consolidation, not a new theorem, empirical study, or novelty determination. Preserve the v0.3 paper and every frozen theory source and artifact.

The manuscript must define the finite-pool estimand and unrestricted zero-path comparator, prove the hypergeometric lower bound and arbitrary-grid theorem completely, prove the counterexample, and distinguish the established NEGEXP design from the candidate special-family result. Include the observed-case protocol amendment, exact deterministic evidence, the analytical nonzero-loss power limitation, unresolved inspection-game priority, and substantial AI provenance. Do not imply human verification or fellowship competitiveness.

Use only installed rendering libraries. No downloads, paid resources, model inference, outreach, or new large allocations. Publish sources, PDF, build instructions, and an integrity manifest in a new paper/sharp-cost directory. Keep added storage below 2 GB.

## Acceptance

- Complete proofs reviewed against the frozen notes; numeric claims checked against saved JSON.
- All PDF pages rendered and visually inspected; no clipped equations or unreadable tables.
- Manifest verifies source, PDF, frozen evidence, and renderer dependencies; PDF rebuild is not advertised as byte-identical unless tested.
- Existing source pins, old-paper manifest, local links, and repository checks remain valid.
- Public v0.7.1 release identifies the manuscript as a consolidation and keeps the overall research goal active.
