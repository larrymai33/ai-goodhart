# Current finite-cost manuscript design

The strongest current contribution candidate is the all-grid finite minimax
characterization and its cached upper construction, including the subcensus
dyadic rate. The distributed PDFs stop at v0.3 and v0.7, and the review guide
still identifies v0.8 as latest. A reader must traverse several notes to
check the current proof. Consolidate the existing result into a current,
self-contained research manuscript; do not market consolidation as new science.

The main text should define the fixed-label experiment and exact validity,
completion, cost, and supplied-margin quantifiers before stating
`F/2^38 <= C* <= 2^16 F`, the separated-grid `17F` bound, and the dyadic
subcensus corollary. Explain why clipped envelope cost and deterministic
tail truncation both appear. Attribute the generic mechanisms, including
the newly inspected weighted-query talk's deterministic tail truncation.

Complete proof appendices must include the all-grid lower proof, the cached
upper proof, and finite-saturation Sections 2-6. The hidden-subset argument
in Section 3 is needed for small prompt counts; omitting it leaves a gap.
Expand the maximal residual-row argument with its moment-generating-function
inequality. Remove duplicated introductions and irrelevant release history,
while preserving every assumption, proof branch, constant, and completion
mechanism. Keep an explicit source/dependency map against frozen originals.

Treat v0.14's finite-instance improvement as a secondary example with a link
to its separate complete proof. Do not label that secondary linked proof
self-contained in this manuscript. Distinguish one all-pass assertion from
selectable bands, correct fixed labels from a validated grader, and ideal
real-label constructions from rational executable references. Disclose
Codex assistance and lack of independent human review or established priority.

Use a new folder `paper/finite-cost/`; preserve old manuscript and scientific
source bytes. Supply Markdown source, a PDF built with existing local
libraries, reproduction instructions, and a hash manifest. Use only installed
software, no downloads, inference, outreach, or >=2 GB added storage.
Inspect every PDF page visually and verify equations remain legible. Update
the review guide and README to lead with current claims and their evidence.

Success for this phase means a current inspectable artifact with complete
proof dependencies and faithful attribution, not field impact or fellowship
competitiveness. A future extension requires a concrete structural or
practical gap; more small constants alone do not pass that test.
